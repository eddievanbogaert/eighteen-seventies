# /notes

Thinking, not writing. Everything unverified lives here or in `/research`,
never in `/chapters` (rule 3 in `AGENTS.md`).

What belongs here:

- Structural and editorial decisions, and the reasoning behind them.
- Conventions the whole book must apply consistently: romanisation, name
  order, place names, calendars, currency.
- Records of historiographical disagreement, worked out at length before the
  chapter compresses them.
- Half-formed connections between threads, recall that needs checking, and
  ideas that may not survive.
- Presentism holding pen: present-day parallels that are tempting, kept here
  until it is clear whether they are framing worth using or arguments
  masquerading as framing.

There is no format requirement. Being wrong in this directory costs nothing,
which is the point of having it.
