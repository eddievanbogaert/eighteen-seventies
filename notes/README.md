# /notes

Thinking, not writing. Everything unverified lives here or in `/research`,
never in `/chapters` (rule 3 in `AGENTS.md`).

What belongs here:

- Structural and editorial decisions, and the reasoning behind them.
- Conventions the whole book must apply consistently: romanisation, name
  order, place names, calendars, currency.
- Records of historiographical disagreement, worked out at length before the
  chapter compresses them.
- Half-formed connections between threads, recall that needs checking, and
  ideas that may not survive.
- Presentism holding pen: present-day parallels that are tempting, kept here
  until it is clear whether they are framing worth using or arguments
  masquerading as framing.

There is no format requirement. Being wrong in this directory costs nothing,
which is the point of having it.

## Current files

- `REVIEW-QUEUE.md` — generated: the claims awaiting the author's own
  reading, grouped by source and page. Regenerate with
  `python3 tools/review_queue.py --write`.
- `PROGRESS.md` — the resume file: how a chapter gets made, the source rules
  for the drafting phase, current state, and a session log. A session picking
  this project up should read it first.
- `butler-the-two-letters.md` — the two pieces Butler published in the Press
  of Christchurch in 1863 and 1865, which contradict each other, and what
  Erewhon did with them. Chapter 04's argument, and the reason it no longer
  waits on Q04-4 and Q04-5.
- `erewhon-edition-collation.md` — collating the 1872 and 1901 texts of
  Erewhon, and what it settles about the Book of the Machines.
- `historiography-panic-1873.md` — notes on Calandro's article on Grant and
  the panic: what it argues, what it is good for as a finding aid, and what
  may not be taken from it. Records the 1874-75 structural finding.
- `pending-provenance.md` — material received but not yet usable, because
  where it came from is unknown. Nothing listed there may be cited.
- `thesis.md` — the working thesis, what it stopped claiming and why, the
  conventions that follow from it, and the two open author decisions the thesis
  line raises. Start here; it governs the other two.
- `chapter-order.md` — the chronology, why chapters sit where they do, and the
  ordering questions still open.
- `voice-and-shape.md` — audience, register, what a finished chapter looks
  like, the length budget, and where to start drafting. Editorial proposals,
  none of them binding.
- `question-triage.md` — all 121 questions in `/research/QUESTIONS.md` sorted
  by what they block, the seven that are thesis-critical, and a suggested
  critical path. Goes stale as questions close; `QUESTIONS.md` is authoritative.
