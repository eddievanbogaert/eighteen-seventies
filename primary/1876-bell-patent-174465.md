## Provenance

- **Document:** Letters Patent No. 174,465, *Improvement in Telegraphy*,
  Alexander Graham Bell, of Salem, Massachusetts. United States Patent Office.
- **Dates as printed on the document:** specification "dated March 7, 1876;
  application filed February 14, 1876". The specification's own signature
  block is dated "this 20th day of January, A. D. 1876".
- **Copy transcribed from:** a 6-page PDF of the printed patent, 2 drawing
  sheets and 4 specification pages, page images at 2320 × 3408 px.
- **How the copy reached this repository:** retrieved by the author from
  `patentimages.storage.googleapis.com/10/ab/a3/b4f5c6e5826640/US174465.pdf`
  and passed to this session, 2026-11-13. 565,802 bytes, sha256
  `300bb898a4981877b8901e78fcbd89e3b6fd571c59e4dcadb9c6feee0334aa8e`.
- **Form:** page images. Everything below was read from the rendered images,
  not from OCR. This matters: the Google Patents OCR of this same patent
  renders claim 2 in garbled form, and an earlier reading of that OCR would
  have put a corrupted claim into the record.
- **Rights:** a United States patent of 1876. No rights statement appears on
  the document.
- **Transcribed by:** Claude (assistant editor), 2026-11-13.

## Readings recorded as uncertain

The witness signatures on the two drawing sheets are handwritten and were not
confidently legible at the resolution available. They are **not** transcribed
rather than guessed at. The witnesses to the specification, which is set in
type, are legible and appear in the claims file.

## Earlier filings the patent itself cites

Both are referred to in the specification's opening recitals and are recorded
here as pointers, not as verified facts about those documents:

- Letters Patent granted to Bell **April 6, 1875, No. 161,739**.
- A pending application filed **February 25, 1875**.

Neither has been retrieved. Each needs its own row before anything about it
enters a chapter.
