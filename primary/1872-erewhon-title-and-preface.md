**Transcription only.** Commentary belongs in `/notes`.

## Provenance

- **Document:** *Erewhon, or Over the Range*. London: Trübner & Co., 60
  Paternoster Row, 1872. First edition.
- **Copy transcribed from:** Internet Archive item `erewhonoroverra00butl`,
  scanned from the Duke University Libraries copy (bookplate: "The Glenn
  Negley Collection of Utopian Literature"). Page images, sha256
  `29081ced6a197cac…`, retrieved 2026-11-13. 264 images.
- **Form:** page images, read as images. The Internet Archive's OCR of the
  same item (`erewhonoroverra00butl_djvu.txt`, sha256 `fa60789ed3116c8c…`)
  was used for searching and counting only; both passages below were checked
  against the image.
- **Transcribed by:** Claude (assistant editor), 2026-11-13.

**Why this file exists.** `/primary/1901-butler-preface-erewhon.md` holds the
revised edition, in which Butler says he added about sixty pages and blended
them so the seams would not show. Chapter 04 is about 1872. These are the
1872 pages.

**No author's name appears on the title page.** Neither "Samuel Butler" nor a
pseudonym nor "by the author of —" is printed there. The Greek epigraph is
set small and is partly faint in this scan; it is given above as it reads,
and a second copy should be consulted before it is quoted in the book.

## Preface, PDF image 9 of 264

This is the whole of it.

> PREFACE.
>
> THE Author wishes it to be understood that Erewhon is pronounced as a word
> of three syllables, all short—thus, Ĕ-rĕ-whŏn.

The first two words are set in small capitals. The breve accents over the
three vowels are printed and are reproduced here; the Internet Archive's OCR
drops them.

## Notes on the transcription, not about history

- Image numbers are positions in the cached PDF of the scan, not printed page
  numbers. The title page and the preface are both unnumbered in the book.
- The contents list, on the page numbered viii (PDF image 12 of 264), gives
  the three machine chapters as XXI "The Book of the Machines", XXII "The
  Machines—continued", XXIII "The Machines—concluded", at printed pages 190,
  198 and 209. Those printed numbers are read from the contents list, which
  is itself a claim by the printer; they have not been checked against the
  chapter openings.
